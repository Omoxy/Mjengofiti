# Mjengofiti — Kenyan Regulatory and Standards Register

## 1. Purpose and Statutory Scope
The Standards Register documents verified regulatory references from Kenyan statutory authorities that govern building construction, structural compliance, and development control in Nairobi County.

The platform never invents statutory requirements or claims an automated screening indicator constitutes a legal determination of non-compliance. Every codified rule cites an authoritative statute, gazette notice, or code section.

---

## 2. Authoritative Registers

### 2.1 National Building Code 2024 (Kenya Gazette)
- **Authority:** Ministry of Lands, Public Works, Housing and Urban Development / National Construction Authority (NCA)
- **Publication Reference:** National Building Code 2024 / Kenya Gazette Supplement
- **Status:** Statutory Code / Regulatory Reference
- **Key Clauses:**
  - **Section B.4 — Structural Safety & Stability:** Every building structure must resist all dead, imposed, and wind loads without exceeding permissible deflection or stress limits.
  - **Section B.12 — Drainage and Flood Protection:** Site preparations must ensure storm water runoff does not compromise foundation integrity or saturate adjacent building subsoils.
  - **Section C.3 — Building Height & Setbacks:** Minimum horizontal setbacks from road reserves, neighbouring boundary lines, and riparian corridors as specified by county spatial development plans.
  - **Section F.2 — Fire Safety & Means of Escape:** Minimum stairwell width, external egress routes, and fire suppression access requirements based on occupancy classification.

### 2.2 National Construction Authority Act (No. 41 of 2011)
- **Authority:** National Construction Authority (NCA)
- **Key Sections:**
  - **Section 15 — Site Registration:** All construction projects within Kenya exceeding threshold value must be registered with the Authority prior to commencement.
  - **Section 16 — Accreditation of Skilled Construction Workers & Site Supervisors:** Mandatory on-site qualified supervisory personnel.
  - **Section 23 — Enforcement & Closure Notices:** Power to issue suspension orders and closure notices for unsafe, unregistered, or structurally suspect construction sites.

### 2.3 Physical and Land Use Planning Act (No. 13 of 2019)
- **Authority:** County Government of Nairobi / National Land Commission
- **Key Sections:**
  - **Section 56 — Development Permission:** Unauthorised development without approved architectural and structural engineering drawings is an offence.
  - **Section 69 — Riparian Reserve Protection:** Strict prohibition of permanent building structures within declared riparian reserves (minimum 30m buffer from riverbanks of Nairobi, Mathare, and Ngong rivers).

### 2.4 Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA 2007)
- **Authority:** Directorate of Occupational Safety and Health Services (DOSHS)
- **Key Sections:**
  - **Section 47 — Structural Soundness of Workplaces:** Buildings used for manufacturing, commercial activity, or public congregation must maintain certified structural soundness.

## 3. Software Enforcement & Mapping in Mjengofiti
- Indicators flag buildings in close proximity to riparian corridors (< 30m) as **Screening Candidates for Environmental Review** referencing PLUPA 2019 Section 69.
- Unregistered construction or missing approvals in official records are categorized as **Information Unavailable / Unverified** rather than an automatic violation notice.
- Inspections conducted under Mjengofiti record the specific statutory clause invoked when a finding of non-compliance is authored by an authorized inspector.
